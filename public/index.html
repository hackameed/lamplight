Lamplight

measures whether your room can be read

Sit down and hold still.

Lamplight reads your pulse and breathing from the webcam, and tells you what to change when the room gets in the way.

  • Face the screen with your upper body visible
  • Pulse takes about 12 seconds, breathing about 30
  • If it can't read you, it will say why — and never make a number up
